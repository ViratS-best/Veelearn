The short answer

Brilliant is excellent at building intuition with short problems. Veelearn starts one step earlier — teaching the skill — and finishes later, with mastery-tracked practice.

Taught before tested

Veelearn opens every skill with a written lesson and worked examples, then hands you questions built from that lesson — so the first card isn't a guess.

The AI is not an upsell

Brilliant's Koji needs Premium. Vee is included: ask for the next step or a similar worked example and get a specific answer, free.

Practice that moves with you

Four mastery levels per skill. A wrong answer steps the next card down a tier; a unit stays open until you can do it at level 3.

Where Brilliant is genuinely strong

  • Beautifully made interactive problems that make ideas feel intuitive.
  • A daily habit: short sessions that are easy to come back to.
  • Good coverage of maths, science and computer science thinking.
  • No passive videos — you're solving something every minute.

Where Veelearn goes further

  • 100% free, including the AI tutor — no keys, no paywall.
  • It teaches the skill in full before you're tested on it.
  • Questions are written from that lesson, not a fixed course bank.
  • Mastery gates mean a finished unit means something.
  • Simulations and a board that draw your actual question.

Our take: if you like Brilliant's solve-it-yourself style, you'll like Veelearn's practice too — but Veelearn explains the method first, adapts the questions to your level, and doesn't charge for the tutor.

What a Veelearn session feels like

Taught, then practised, then mastered — in one place.

1. Read the lesson

Every skill opens with a written lesson: the idea, a method you can reuse, several worked examples and the traps. The last example matches the hardest card you'll get.

2. Practise with Vee

One card at a time, with a specific hint ladder. Ask Vee to explain a step or show a twin example and it teaches — it doesn't just hand over the answer.

3. Close the skill

Your mastery circle fills as you answer. A unit closes at level 3 with enough correct cards, so the progress you see is real.

Brilliant questions, answered

Is Brilliant free?

Brilliant has a free tier limited to 2 keys a day, with ads and only a short preview of its AI tutor, Koji. Full access to Koji, unlimited practice and an ad-free experience need Brilliant Premium.

How is Veelearn different from Brilliant?

Both make you solve problems instead of only watching videos. Veelearn also writes a full lesson for every skill first — the idea, a reusable method, worked examples and common mistakes — then builds a tiered question sequence from that lesson. The AI tutor and every question are free.

Is Veelearn really 100% free?

Yes. Veelearn is a nonprofit, fiscally sponsored by Hack Club, so learning is 100% free and donations are optional.

Does Veelearn have interactive simulations?

Yes. Veelearn includes 120+ interactive PhET simulations, and its session board draws the specific question you're on, with sliders you can move to see how it changes.
