The short answer

Khan Academy is a genuinely great free library. Veelearn is built around the part that comes after watching: being taught, then practising until it sticks.

Free, including the tutor

Where Khan Academy is genuinely strong

  • A huge video library across maths, science, economics and the humanities.
  • Trusted by schools and parents, with years of content behind it.
  • Khanmigo is free for teachers, which is a real win for classrooms.
  • Great as a reference when you want to watch one topic explained.

Where Veelearn goes further

  • The AI tutor is free, not a $4/month add-on.
  • It teaches the exact skill you're on before it tests you.
  • Questions are written from that lesson, not pulled from a fixed bank.
  • Mastery gates keep a unit open until you can actually do it.
  • The board draws your question; it doesn't show you a stock graph.

Our take: if you want to watch a topic explained, Khan Academy is excellent. If you want to be taught a skill and then drilled on it until it's yours — for free — Veelearn is the better fit.

What a Veelearn session feels like

No playlist to guess at. One skill at a time, taught and then practised.

1. Read the lesson

Every skill opens with a written lesson: what it is, the method, three to six worked examples and the mistakes to avoid. The last example is the same kind of question as your hardest practice card.

2. Practise with Vee

One card at a time. Stuck? Ask for the next step or a similar example and get a specific answer — not a generic "re-read the question". Hints name the next move.

3. Close the skill

Your mastery circle fills from 1 to 4. A unit ends when you've answered enough cards correctly at level 3, so the tick means something.

Khan Academy questions, answered

Is Veelearn free like Khan Academy?

Yes. Veelearn is a nonprofit and 100% free, including the AI tutor, the lessons, the practice and the simulations. Khan Academy's courses are also free, but its AI tutor Khanmigo costs $4 a month or $44 a year for learners and families.

How is Veelearn different from Khanmigo?

Khanmigo sits on top of Khan Academy's existing video and exercise library. Veelearn's AI writes a lesson for the exact skill you're on, with worked examples at the difficulty of your practice card, then builds the question sequence around that lesson and your mastery level.

Can I use Veelearn and Khan Academy together?

Absolutely. Use Khan Academy for its huge video library, and use Veelearn when you want to be taught a skill and then drilled on it until it's mastered. Both are free to start.

Does Veelearn have simulations?

Yes. Veelearn includes 120+ interactive PhET simulations, and the session board can draw the exact situation in your current question, with sliders you can move.
