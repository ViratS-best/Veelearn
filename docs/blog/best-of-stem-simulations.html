Best of STEM Simulations: Complete Comparison Guide for 2026

April 12, 2026 • 14 min read • Education

Interactive STEM simulations transform abstract concepts into tangible experiences. This comprehensive guide compares the best STEM simulation platforms of 2026, helping you choose the perfect tools for physics, chemistry, biology, and mathematics exploration.

Top STEM Simulation Platforms Compared

1. PhET Interactive Simulations

Best for: Free, research-based simulations across all STEM subjects

PhET offers 120+ free, research-based simulations developed at the University of Colorado Boulder. Research shows students using PhET develop 40% deeper conceptual understanding. Completely free, no account required, works on all devices.

2. Gizmos by ExploreLearning

Best for: School districts needing comprehensive curriculum-aligned simulations

Gizmos provides 400+ interactive simulations aligned with state and NGSS standards. Includes student exploration sheets, teacher guides, and assessment tools. Requires school subscription but offers extensive curriculum integration.

3. Veelearn Simulator Studio

Best for: Creating custom simulations with block-based programming

Veelearn integrates PhET simulations with a block-based programming engine for creating custom simulations. Students can fork, remix, and share simulations. The marketplace allows community-created content. Free to use.

4. LabXchange

Best for: Lab simulations with integration to real-world research

LabXchange from Harvard University offers virtual labs connected to real-world research. Includes interactive pathways, digital assets, and community features. Free for educators and students.
