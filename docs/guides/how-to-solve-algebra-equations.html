How to Solve Algebra Equations: Complete Step-by-Step Guide

April 12, 2026 • 13 min read • Guide

Algebra equations are the foundation of mathematical problem-solving. This comprehensive guide teaches you how to solve algebra equations step by step, covering linear equations, quadratic equations, systems of equations, and word problems.

The Golden Rule

Whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other. This maintains equality. If you add 5 to the left, add 5 to the right. If you multiply the left by 3, multiply the right by 3.

Step-by-Step Method

Step 1: Simplify Both Sides

Combine like terms and distribute multiplication. This simplification makes solving easier.

Step 2: Isolate the Variable Term

Move variable terms to one side, constants to the other. Add or subtract terms as needed.

Step 3: Isolate the Variable

Divide by the coefficient. For squared variables, take square roots (include both positive and negative).

Step 4: Check Your Solution

Substitute back into the original equation to verify. This catches errors.
