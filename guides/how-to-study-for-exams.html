How to Study for Exams: Complete Step-by-Step Guide

April 12, 2026 • 12 min read • Guide

Effective exam preparation requires more than just reading notes. This comprehensive guide teaches you research-backed study techniques including active recall, spaced repetition, and systematic review to maximize your exam performance.

Research-Backed Study Techniques

Active Recall

Test yourself rather than re-reading. Close notes and recall information. Use flashcards and practice problems. Active recall strengthens memory 40% more effectively than passive review.

Spaced Repetition

Review at increasing intervals: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month. This optimizes memory retention by reviewing just before forgetting.

Interleaving Practice

Mix different problem types rather than doing all of one type. This forces your brain to distinguish between problem types and choose appropriate strategies.
