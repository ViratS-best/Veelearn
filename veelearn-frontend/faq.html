Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Veelearn's free STEM learning platform.

Is Veelearn completely free?

Yes, Veelearn is completely free to use. All simulations, courses, and features are available at no cost. We believe in making STEM education accessible to everyone.

How do I earn volunteer hours on Veelearn?

You can earn volunteer hours by creating educational content such as courses, simulations, or tutorials. Each approved contribution earns you documented volunteer hours that can be used for school requirements or college applications.

What PhET simulations are available on Veelearn?

Veelearn provides access to 120+ PhET Interactive Simulations covering physics, chemistry, biology, and mathematics. These include mechanics, electricity, magnetism, waves, optics, quantum mechanics, chemical reactions, the periodic table, and more.

Can I create my own courses on Veelearn?

Yes, Veelearn includes a Course Creator tool that allows you to build interactive courses with videos, text, quizzes, and embedded simulations. Your courses can be shared with the community through our marketplace.

How do I use the block-based programming to create simulations?

Veelearn's Simulator Studio provides a block-based programming engine similar to Scratch. You can drag and drop blocks to create custom physics simulations without writing code. The system handles physics calculations and rendering automatically.

Is Veelearn suitable for K-12 students?

Absolutely. Veelearn is designed for K-12 students, college learners, and lifelong learners. Our content is organized by grade level and subject area, making it easy to find age-appropriate material.

Can teachers use Veelearn in the classroom?

Yes, Veelearn offers teacher tools including course assignment, student progress tracking, and simulation sharing. Teachers can create custom courses aligned with their curriculum and monitor student engagement.

Do I need to create an account to use Veelearn?

You can explore simulations and browse courses without an account. However, creating an account is required to take courses, track progress, earn volunteer hours, and create content.

What subjects does Veelearn cover?

Veelearn covers physics, chemistry, mathematics, and biology. Our interactive simulations and courses span from basic concepts to advanced topics suitable for college-level study.

How can I get help if I have technical issues?

For technical support, you can reach out through our contact form or check our help documentation. Our community forum is also available for peer support and questions about using the platform.

Can't find what you're looking for? Explore our guides or contact us directly.
