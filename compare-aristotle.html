Where Aristotle is genuinely strong

  • A live voice conversation, which feels close to a human tutor.
  • Socratic questioning: it won't just hand you the answer.
  • Works from the exact homework problem in front of you.
  • Session summaries and a parent dashboard on paid plans.

Where Veelearn goes further

  • Free, with no session count and no monthly fee.
  • A full skill map, so you cover the subject rather than one problem.
  • Practice generated from each lesson, adapting to your mastery.
  • Simulations and a board for the exact question you're on.
  • Free classroom tools and volunteer hours for creators.

Our take: Aristotle is a strong voice tutor if you want to talk a problem through and can pay monthly. Veelearn is the better choice for steady, week-by-week learning — because it's a complete, adaptive curriculum and it costs nothing.

What a Veelearn session feels like

Free, structured, and built to get you to mastery.

1. Read the lesson

Each skill opens with a written lesson: the idea, a reusable method, worked examples at your card's difficulty, and the mistakes to avoid.

2. Practise with Vee

One card at a time. Ask for the next step or a similar example and Vee teaches it, with a specific hint instead of a generic nudge.

3. Close the skill

Mastery fills from 1 to 4, and a unit only closes at level 3 with enough correct answers. No session meter, no upsell.

Aristotle questions, answered

How much does Aristotle cost?

Aristotle's public pricing lists a free Intro with three one-hour sessions, then Scholar at $49 a month for eight sessions and Infinite at $199 a month for unlimited sessions.

Is Veelearn free?

Yes. Veelearn is a nonprofit, fiscally sponsored by Hack Club, so its AI tutor, lessons, practice and simulations are 100% free. Donations are optional and support keeping it free.

Does Veelearn have a voice tutor like Aristotle?

No. Veelearn's tutor is text and board based, with read-aloud support, rather than a live voice call. It teaches each skill in writing, draws the situation in your question, and coaches you through practice.

Which is better for learning a subject end to end?

Aristotle is designed around tutoring sessions where you bring a problem. Veelearn is built as a course map: units, skills, a written lesson for each skill, tiered practice and mastery tracking, all free. For steady week-by-week learning, Veelearn covers more ground for $0.
